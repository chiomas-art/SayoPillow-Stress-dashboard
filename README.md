🛌💤 SaYoPillow: Sleep & Stress Dashboard
Can a pillow read your mind? Tracking the overnight physiological signals that predict tomorrow’s stress.

✨ The Story Behind the Data
Stress is usually measured reactively—we notice we are stressed only after it has already hijacked our day[cite: 11]. But what if your body gives away the secret hours before you even wake up?

The SaYoPillow project flips the script on wellness tracking[cite: 11]. By analyzing overnight physiological signals captured by smart bedding—such as snoring range, breathing rate, body temperature, limb movement, blood oxygen, REM sleep, hours slept, and heart rate—this project predicts a person's upcoming daily stress level before they've consciously registered it[cite: 11].

Rather than keeping these 630 overnight sleep records locked away in static files[cite: 11], I built an interactive, warm-toned dashboard that lets you tweak your own sleep telemetry sliders in real-time, inspect feature correlations, and test predictive machine learning models[cite: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10].

🚀 What’s Inside the Dashboard?
The application is structured into an immersive exploration of sleep science and predictive modeling:

🎛️ Live Stress Predictor (Sidebar Sliders): Move the sliders for snoring, respiration rate, body temperature, blood oxygen, REM sleep, sleep hours, and heart rate to see your predicted stress state update instantly on the fly[cite: 5, 7, 8].

📊 Your Readings vs. The Typical Sleeper: Compare your custom sleep metrics visually against baseline populations using standardized deviation charts[cite: 6].

🔬 Stress Level Mix & Signal Correlations: Explore the balance across 5 stress classes (from Low/Normal to High) and visualize the heavy-hitting correlation matrix where physiological signals link directly to stress outcomes[cite: 9, 11].

⚙️ Model Performance & Feature Importances: Evaluate machine learning prowess—featuring a 100.0% accuracy Logistic Regression model and a 98.4% accuracy Random Forest model, complete with breakdown bar charts showing which vital signs drive the algorithm[cite: 10, 11].

🛠️ Built With
Frontend & Interactivity: Streamlit[cite: 5]

Data Processing & Analysis: Python, Pandas, NumPy[cite: 11]

Data Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn[cite: 9, 10]

Predictive Modeling: Scikit-Learn (Logistic Regression & Random Forest classifiers)[cite: 10, 11]
